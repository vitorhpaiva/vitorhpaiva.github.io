i.

Research

Four threads run through the group's work. They are not separate projects — the birds carry them between them.

Foraging strategies & ecological roles

How breeding seabirds decide where to go, how far, and what for — and what those choices say about where a colony sits in the food web.

Trophic relationships in marine communities

Working outward from top predators to the wider community — competition, dependency, and the slow reshuffling that climate and fisheries drive.

Seabirds as sentinels

What birds eat, where they feed, and how well their chicks grow are early signals of ocean health, and of the human pressures the ocean cannot absorb.

Biologging, modelling, statistics

The toolkit I build with: GPS loggers, stable isotopes, movement models, ecosystem simulations, and the statistics that keep the inferences honest.
